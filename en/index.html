Quick qualification

Is your project a good fit?

Your project is probably a good fit if…

  • ✓ a repetitive task regularly takes up team time;
  • ✓ information must be found across documents or tools;
  • ✓ people repeatedly copy, check or rewrite data;
  • ✓ you want a concrete first result quickly;
  • ✓ someone can validate the agent’s answers.

Your project is probably not a good fit if…

  • — you expect fully autonomous AI without supervision;
  • — the need has not been identified yet;
  • — nobody can take part in validation;
  • — the project immediately requires complex critical infrastructure;
  • — you expect a guaranteed commercial outcome.

Common questions before getting started

What type of task can be automated?

A precise, repetitive task based on rules or documents, with an output that a person can validate.

How do I know whether my project is a good fit?

We check task frequency, available data, users, human review and the expected output.

Why does the price start at €4,990 excluding VAT?

The price covers scoping, design, testing, documentation and onboarding. The final price depends on sources, integrations, users and automation.

When does the 15-business-day target start?

After scope approval, receipt of the required access and official project launch.

What costs should I expect after delivery?

They may include software, API usage, hosting, automation and optional maintenance. Known costs are agreed before work starts.
